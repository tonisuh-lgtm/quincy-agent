9 Quincy Property Management, operated by Eastring LLC (owner: Dongyeon Suh), provides residential property management services for tenants at 9 Quincy Pl NE #2, Washington DC 20002.

SMS Communications

We send transactional SMS messages to current tenants for the following purposes:

  • Monthly rent payment reminders
  • Maintenance request updates and coordination
  • Scheduling notifications for property services (cleaning, pest control)
  • General property announcements

How Tenants Opt In

Tenants provide written consent to receive SMS communications by signing a standalone SMS Consent Form addendum to their lease agreement. The form includes a dedicated field for the tenant's mobile phone number, message frequency disclosure, message and data rates disclosure, STOP and HELP instructions, and an unchecked checkbox requiring active consent. Consent is voluntary and not a condition of tenancy.

Message Frequency

Approximately 4 to 8 messages per month, varying based on property needs.

Opt-Out Instructions

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving SMS communications. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.
